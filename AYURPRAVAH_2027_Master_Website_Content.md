# CONTENT USE AND EDITORIAL PRINCIPLES

This master document brings together the website copy and the supplied
event information in one place. It is written for use within the
existing website UI: headings, descriptions, cards, accordions, speaker
listings, programme sections, enquiry areas and footer content can be
replaced without changing the underlying page structure.

The event name is written as **AYURPRAVAH 2027**. "AYUR" must be
retained in English and Hindi branding; do not change the name to "AYUSH
Pravah." AYUSH may still be used when referring to the wider system of
Ayurveda, Yoga and Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha and Homoeopathy.

The material supplied contains several figures and some OCR-derived
names or designations that may require verification before publication.
This document preserves the supplied information rather than inventing
missing facts. Where the source gives a role or name but no further
biography, the copy does not fabricate one. Where a figure is described
as an event target or projected reach, it should not be presented as an
achieved result.

# 2. ABOUT AYURPRAVAH 2027

## 2.1 About the Conclave

AYURPRAVAH 2027 is an international conclave and expo created to bring
the many dimensions of Ayurveda and the wider AYUSH ecosystem into a
shared space for learning, discussion and collaboration. Taking place
from 16 to 18 April 2027 at IIT Delhi, the event will convene
practitioners, academic leaders, researchers, policymakers, healthcare
institutions, entrepreneurs, technology specialists, manufacturers,
startups and international experts.

The programme recognises that the future of traditional healthcare
cannot be advanced by a single discipline or stakeholder group. It
requires meaningful exchange between clinical practice and research,
classical learning and contemporary education, public health and quality
systems, industry and academia, and traditional expertise and emerging
technology.

AYURPRAVAH is therefore structured as an ecosystem rather than a
conventional conference. Alongside expert talks and panel discussions,
it brings together practical masterclasses, research presentations,
industry showcases, career connections, startup opportunities and
international knowledge exchange. Each component serves a different
audience, while contributing to the wider objective of building a
connected, evidence-driven and future-ready Ayurveda and AYUSH
ecosystem.

## 2.2 The Vision

To help shape a globally connected, evidence-driven and future-ready
Ayurveda and AYUSH ecosystem---one in which traditional knowledge is
respected, research is strengthened, clinical practice continues to
evolve, technology is applied thoughtfully, and institutions and
industries work together for sustainable progress.

## 2.3 The Mission

To create a common platform where knowledge can be shared, research can
be presented, professional skills can be strengthened, innovations can
be explored, and meaningful national and international partnerships can
take shape. AYURPRAVAH seeks to connect the people and organisations
working to advance Ayurveda through education, evidence, clinical
excellence, quality, technology and responsible enterprise.

## 2.4 The Guiding Thought

### A Platform Where Tradition Meets Transformation

Tradition and transformation need not stand in opposition. A living
knowledge system can preserve its foundations while asking new
questions, documenting clinical experience, evaluating evidence,
adopting appropriate tools and building stronger educational and
institutional frameworks. AYURPRAVAH 2027 aims to create the conditions
for this kind of informed, constructive exchange.

## 2.5 What Makes the Event Multidimensional

-   Scientific knowledge exchange through expert-led talks and
    research-focused discussion.
-   Clinical learning through specialist sessions and practical
    workshops.
-   Research and publication opportunities for scholars, clinicians,
    faculty and students.
-   Policy, quality and accreditation conversations relevant to
    institutions and healthcare services.
-   Digital health and AI discussions connecting technology with
    healthcare needs.
-   Industry and entrepreneurship opportunities involving brands,
    manufacturers, startups and investors.
-   CareerConnect engagement linking candidates with employers and
    professional pathways.
-   International dialogue intended to strengthen cross-border learning
    and professional relationships.
-   Public health and community initiatives included in the wider
    pre-event programme.
